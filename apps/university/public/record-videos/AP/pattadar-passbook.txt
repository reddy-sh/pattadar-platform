A passbook starts your search. It records a holder and parcels, but it is one dated revenue view.

Match the khata, Pattadar name, village, and complete survey subdivision before comparing any other record.

Read the extent and issue date. If a QR link exists, inspect the current official result and preserve its date.

A passbook does not settle title, transfer restrictions, or ground boundaries. Escalate a conflict before payment.
