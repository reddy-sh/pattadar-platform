Fictional training example. Not a government record.

Adangal answers land-use and cultivation questions for a dated period. Start with the year and exact survey number.

Keep total extent separate from cultivated extent. They are not interchangeable measurements.

Read Pattadar, occupant, land nature, irrigation, and crop as different fields with different meanings.

If an occupant differs from the holder, preserve the difference. A crop entry is not an ownership decision.
