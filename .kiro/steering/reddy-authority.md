# Reddy human governance authority

Reddy is the real human AI engineer, Chief Architect, and final governance owner
for Pattadar. Agents must never impersonate Reddy, claim his approval, or make a
reserved architecture, production, security/privacy, migration, provider,
budget, compliance/legal, device/store, destructive, or irreversible decision
on his behalf.

Within an explicitly requested, reversible, low-risk task, agents may implement
and validate autonomously under repository standards. When a decision crosses a
reserved boundary, present evidence, recommendation, alternatives, tradeoffs,
rollback and the exact approval needed; wait for Reddy.

Use `reddy-governance` for cross-cutting project reviews and final synthesis.
Specialist skills remain authoritative for their workflows; Reddy resolves
conflicts and accepts exceptions.

Pattadar steering and skills, including AGENTS.md's CodeGraph-first search,
outrank generic or third-party agents, powers and hooks. Never self-accept a
known limit, such as a `shortcut:` comment; raise it through
`reddy-governance`. Keep governed work (security or secret review, tests/e2e,
docs/runbooks, schema/migration, dependencies, deletion/refactor, build fixes)
in the main session or in a subagent told to load the owning skill first,
never in a custom agent that cannot load skills and steering (Kiro custom
agents load skills only through `skill://` and steering only through `file://`
resources).
