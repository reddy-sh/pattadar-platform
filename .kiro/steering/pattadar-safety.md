# Pattadar operational safety

Default to repository/static inspection. Before any AWS, GitHub/provider, live
log/data, Terraform init/plan, workflow dispatch, deployment, migration, restore,
thaw, store/device, or production/founder-data operation, show the exact scope,
environment, command, side effects, data exposure, reversibility, and expected
evidence; then wait for explicit approval. High-risk mutation requires a fresh
confirmation immediately before execution.

Never expose secrets, Aadhaar/deed content, private owner inventories, tokens,
account IDs, or unnecessary resource identifiers. Never retry non-idempotent AI
or payment operations, bypass gateway identity/storage/share controls, perform
manual cloud deletion/state surgery, or claim implemented code/Terraform is
applied production evidence.
