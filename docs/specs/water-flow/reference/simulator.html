    These lines show where rain water runs on the surface. They do not prove there is groundwater below. Before drilling, get a resistivity survey and ask neighbours how deep their borewells are and how much water they give.

    Lines: D8 flow from each 30 m elevation map, drawn where at least 0.9 ha (2.2 acres) drains through. Red and pink outlines: every 30 m cell whose water flows to that point. Pins: scored on drainage area, lines meeting, and how many of three independent map families (SRTM/NASADEM, AW3D30, Copernicus) have a line within 30 m. Confidence is capped on flat land.
